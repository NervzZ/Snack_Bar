# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Option to count healthstones as health potions, ranked with them by how much they heal
- A click never uses a healthstone and a potion together, and a healthstone on cooldown waits until it is ready

## 1.0.0

First public version.

- Food and water slots that always show the best usable item in your bags
- Optional health and mana potion slots, with backup potions for combat
- Potions on their own bar, or attached to the food and water bar in a line or a square
- Size, gap and position with sliders and numbers you can type
- Show only on mouse hover
- Lock the bars
